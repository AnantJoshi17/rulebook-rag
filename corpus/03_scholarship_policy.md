# Merit Scholarship Policy

**Document ID:** SP
**Version:** 2026.1
**Approved by:** Finance and Scholarships Committee, 21 March 2026

---

## Section SP-1. Scheme

### SP-1.1 Object
The Merit Scholarship is awarded to recognise sustained academic performance in the B.Tech programme. The scholarship takes the form of a remission of a stated portion of the tuition fee for one academic year.

### SP-1.2 Categories
Three categories of Merit Scholarship are offered:
- **Category A** — remission of one hundred per cent of the tuition fee.
- **Category B** — remission of fifty per cent of the tuition fee.
- **Category C** — remission of twenty-five per cent of the tuition fee.

### SP-1.3 Number of Awards
The number of awards in each category is fixed by the Finance and Scholarships Committee before the commencement of the Odd Semester and is published on the University portal. The Committee is not obliged to make the full number of awards in any year.

### SP-1.4 Scope of Remission
Remission under this policy applies to the tuition fee component only. Examination fees, hostel charges, mess charges, caution deposit and any other charge specified in the Fee Schedule are payable in full by scholarship holders.

---

## Section SP-2. Eligibility and Renewal

### SP-2.1 Renewal Threshold
**A Merit Scholarship awarded in any category shall be renewed for the following academic year only where the holder has obtained a Cumulative Grade Point Average of not less than 8.00 at the close of the preceding academic year.** A holder whose CGPA falls below 8.00 shall cease to hold the scholarship with effect from the commencement of the following academic year.

### SP-2.2 First-Year Award
The Merit Scholarship for the first year is awarded on the basis of the qualifying examination percentage recorded at admission. Category A requires ninety-five per cent or above, Category B requires ninety per cent or above, and Category C requires eighty-five per cent or above.

### SP-2.3 Award in Subsequent Years
For the second, third and fourth years, awards are made on the basis of the CGPA at the close of the preceding academic year, in descending order of CGPA, until the number of awards fixed under SP-1.3 is exhausted.

### SP-2.4 Effect of a Backlog
A student carrying a backlog at the close of an academic year is not eligible for the award or renewal of a Merit Scholarship for the following academic year, irrespective of CGPA.

### SP-2.5 Effect of Disciplinary Action
A student against whom a finding of an examination offence has been recorded under Document EC, or who has been expelled from the hostel under Document HH, is not eligible for the award or renewal of a Merit Scholarship for the following academic year.

### SP-2.6 Restoration
A holder who has ceased to hold a scholarship under SP-2.1 may be considered afresh for an award in a subsequent year under SP-2.3, provided the conditions in SP-2.4 and SP-2.5 are satisfied. Restoration is prospective only and does not carry any arrears.

---

## Section SP-3. Application and Disbursement

### SP-3.1 Application
Applications for the Merit Scholarship shall be submitted on the University portal within the window notified by the Finance and Scholarships Committee, ordinarily in the four weeks following publication of the Even Semester results.

### SP-3.2 Verification
The Office of the Controller of Examinations shall verify the CGPA of each applicant. The Accounts Office shall verify that the applicant has no outstanding dues.

### SP-3.3 Publication of the Award List
The list of scholarship awards shall be published on the University portal not later than two weeks before the commencement of the Odd Semester.

### SP-3.4 Manner of Remission
Remission is applied against the tuition fee demand raised for the year. Where a scholarship is awarded after the tuition fee has been paid in full, the remitted amount shall be adjusted against the demand for the following semester, or refunded where no further demand is expected.

### SP-3.5 Representation
An applicant who is dissatisfied with the outcome may submit a written representation to the Finance and Scholarships Committee within ten working days of publication of the award list. The decision of the Committee on the representation is final.

---

## Section SP-4. Other Financial Assistance

### SP-4.1 Need-Based Assistance
The University operates a separate need-based assistance fund. Applications for need-based assistance are considered by the Finance and Scholarships Committee on the basis of documented family income and are independent of the Merit Scholarship.

### SP-4.2 Sports Scholarship
A student representing the University in an inter-university sporting event may be considered for a Sports Scholarship. Eligibility, categories and the number of awards are determined separately by the Dean of Students in consultation with the Finance and Scholarships Committee.

### SP-4.3 Combination of Awards
A student may not simultaneously hold a Merit Scholarship and a Sports Scholarship. Where a student qualifies for both, the student shall elect one within ten working days of publication of the award lists.

### SP-4.4 External Scholarships
A student holding a scholarship awarded by a government body or an external agency shall declare the award to the Accounts Office. Holding an external scholarship does not by itself disqualify a student from a Merit Scholarship, but the total remission and assistance received shall not exceed the total fee payable for the year.

---

## Section SP-5. Obligations of Holders

### SP-5.1 Continued Registration
A scholarship holder shall remain registered as a full-time student in the B.Tech programme. A holder who withdraws from the programme, or whose registration lapses, ceases to hold the scholarship from the date of withdrawal or lapse.

### SP-5.2 Attendance
A scholarship holder shall satisfy the attendance requirement in Document AR in every registered course. Where a holder is detained in any course for shortage of attendance, the Finance and Scholarships Committee may withdraw the scholarship for the remainder of the academic year.

### SP-5.3 Reporting Changes
A holder shall report to the Accounts Office any change in the fee category, hostel status or external scholarship status within fifteen days of the change.

### SP-5.4 Recovery
Where a scholarship has been awarded on the basis of information later found to be incorrect, the Finance and Scholarships Committee may withdraw the award and recover the remitted amount.
