### FS-3.1 Consequence of Non-Payment
A student whose dues remain unpaid on the date of issue of admit cards shall not be issued an admit card for the end-semester examination, irrespective of attendance. Results of a student with outstanding dues shall be withheld until the dues are cleared.

### FS-3.2 Mode of Payment
Payment shall be made through the University payment portal by net banking, card or UPI. Cash is not accepted at the Accounts Office. A payment is treated as made on the date on which it is credited to the University account.

### FS-3.3 Instalment Relief
A student may apply to the Accounts Office to pay an instalment in two parts, with the second part due not later than thirty days after the original due date. Applications shall be made not later than seven days before the original due date and are granted at the discretion of the Finance Officer.

## Section FS-5. Refunds

### FS-5.1 Withdrawal from the Programme
Refund of fees on withdrawal from the programme is governed by the norms notified by the regulatory authority. Where a student withdraws before the commencement of classes, the tuition fee is refunded in full after deduction of a processing charge of 5,000. Where a student withdraws after the commencement of classes, the tuition fee for the semester in progress is not refundable.

### FS-5.2 Withdrawal from the Hostel
Where a resident withdraws from hostel accommodation under HH-10.1, hostel room rent is refunded on a pro-rata basis for complete unexpired months, after deduction of the notice period. Mess charges are refunded on a pro-rata basis for complete unexpired weeks. The caution deposit is refunded in full on no-dues clearance.

### FS-5.3 Time for Refund
Approved refunds are credited to the bank account from which the payment was made, within thirty working days of approval.

## Section FS-6. Fee Concessions

### FS-6.1 Merit Scholarship Remission
Remission granted under Document SP is applied against the tuition fee head shown in Section FS-1. Remission does not extend to the development fee, laboratory fee, library fee, examination fee, student activity fee, hostel charges or mess charges.

### FS-6.2 Continuation of Remission
**Where a student holds a Merit Scholarship in any category, the remission shall continue into the following academic year provided the student maintains a Cumulative Grade Point Average of not less than 7.50 and has no outstanding dues.** The Accounts Office shall apply the continued remission to the first instalment demand for the following year without requiring a fresh application.

### FS-6.3 Sibling Concession
Where two or more siblings are simultaneously enrolled in a programme of the University, a concession of ten per cent on the tuition fee is available to the sibling with the later date of admission, on production of documentary evidence of relationship.

### FS-6.4 Staff Ward Concession
A ward of a serving member of the University staff who has completed three years of continuous service is eligible for a concession of twenty-five per cent on the tuition fee. The concession is claimed annually through the Human Resources Office.

## Section FS-7. Caution Deposits

### FS-7.1 Programme Caution Deposit
A refundable programme caution deposit of 15,000 is payable at admission and is refunded on completion of the programme, after adjustment of any outstanding dues, library fines or laboratory recoveries.

### FS-7.2 Claim Period
A claim for refund of a caution deposit shall be made within two years of completion of, or withdrawal from, the programme. Deposits unclaimed after two years are transferred to the student welfare fund.
