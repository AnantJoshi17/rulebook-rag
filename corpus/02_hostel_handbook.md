# Hostel Handbook — Residential Rules and Procedures

**Document ID:** HH
**Version:** 2026.1
**Issued by:** Office of the Dean of Students, 2 April 2026
**Applies to:** All students allotted accommodation in a University hostel.

---

## Section HH-1. Allotment

### HH-1.1 Eligibility for Allotment
Hostel accommodation is offered to students whose permanent residential address, as recorded at admission, is situated more than forty kilometres from the University campus. Where rooms remain unallotted after the needs of eligible students have been met, the Dean of Students may allot the remaining rooms to other students.

### HH-1.2 Application
Applications for hostel accommodation shall be submitted on the University portal during the allotment window notified before the commencement of the Odd Semester. Allotment is made for one academic year and does not carry forward automatically.

### HH-1.3 Room Allocation
Rooms are allotted by the Hostel Warden. First-year students are ordinarily allotted shared triple-occupancy rooms. Students in the third and fourth years may apply for double-occupancy rooms subject to availability.

### HH-1.4 Change of Room
A request for change of room shall be made in writing to the Hostel Warden. Room changes are ordinarily considered only once in an academic year and only after the close of the fourth week of the Odd Semester.

### HH-1.5 Vacating
A resident vacating a room shall obtain a no-dues clearance from the Hostel Warden, return the room key and hand over all issued furniture in serviceable condition. Failure to obtain clearance shall result in withholding of the semester result.

---

## Section HH-2. Residents' General Conduct

### HH-2.1 Quiet Hours
Quiet hours are observed in all hostel blocks between 10:30 pm and 6:00 am. Musical instruments, loudspeakers and amplified audio equipment shall not be operated during quiet hours.

### HH-2.2 Prohibited Articles
The following articles are prohibited in hostel rooms: electric heaters, induction cooktops, immersion rods, kettles, refrigerators exceeding fifty litres in capacity, tobacco products, alcohol and any narcotic substance. Prohibited articles found during inspection shall be confiscated.

### HH-2.3 Visitors
Visitors are permitted only in the designated visitors' lounge and only between 9:00 am and 7:00 pm. Visitors are not permitted in residential corridors or rooms at any time. A resident shall record the name of each visitor in the visitors' register maintained at the hostel gate.

### HH-2.4 Room Inspection
The Hostel Warden or a person authorised by the Warden may inspect any hostel room. Inspections are ordinarily conducted with prior notice of twenty-four hours, except where the Warden has reason to believe that a prohibited article is present, in which case inspection may be conducted without notice.

### HH-2.5 Damage to Property
A resident who causes damage to hostel property shall bear the cost of repair or replacement as assessed by the Hostel Warden. Where responsibility for damage to common property cannot be assigned to an individual, the cost shall be recovered equally from the residents of the affected floor.

### HH-2.6 Ragging
Ragging in any form is prohibited. Any incident of ragging shall be reported immediately to the Anti-Ragging Committee. A resident found to have engaged in ragging shall be expelled from the hostel and reported to the Dean of Students for further action under University discipline rules.

---

## Section HH-3. Mess and Dining

### HH-3.1 Mess Membership
Mess membership is compulsory for all hostel residents. Mess charges are payable in advance for the full semester as specified in the Fee Schedule.

### HH-3.2 Meal Timings
Breakfast is served between 7:30 am and 9:30 am, lunch between 12:30 pm and 2:30 pm, and dinner between 7:30 pm and 9:30 pm. Meals are not served outside these hours except to residents holding a sick-diet authorisation issued by the University Health Centre.

### HH-3.3 Mess Rebate
A resident who is absent from the hostel for a continuous period of seven days or more may claim a mess rebate for the period of absence, provided the absence was recorded in the leave register before departure. Rebates are computed at the daily mess rate and are credited at the close of the semester.

### HH-3.4 Food from Outside
Residents may bring packaged food into the hostel. Cooking in hostel rooms is prohibited under HH-2.2.

---

## Section HH-4. Leave and Absence from the Hostel

### HH-4.1 Application for Leave
**A resident intending to remain absent from the hostel overnight shall apply for leave in the hostel leave register not less than forty-eight (48) hours before the intended departure.** The application shall state the destination, the purpose of the visit and the expected date and time of return. Leave is granted at the discretion of the Hostel Warden.

### HH-4.2 Parental Consent for First-Year Residents
A first-year resident shall not be granted overnight leave without written consent from a parent or legal guardian recorded on the University portal. Consent recorded once at the beginning of the academic year is valid for the year.

### HH-4.3 Late Return
A resident who returns after the expiry of sanctioned leave shall report to the Hostel Warden immediately on return and shall submit a written explanation. Repeated late return may result in withdrawal of leave privileges for the remainder of the semester.

### HH-4.4 Day Absence
A resident who leaves the campus and returns the same day before the gate closing hour is not required to apply for leave, but shall record departure and return in the gate register.

### HH-4.5 Gate Closing Hour
The hostel gate closes at 10:00 pm. A resident who has not returned by the gate closing hour and who does not hold sanctioned leave shall be recorded as an unauthorised absentee and shall be reported to the Hostel Warden.

---

## Section HH-5. Health and Safety

### HH-5.1 University Health Centre
The University Health Centre operates between 8:00 am and 8:00 pm on working days and maintains an on-call medical officer outside those hours. Residents shall report illness to the Health Centre.

### HH-5.2 Notification of Illness
A resident who is unwell shall inform the Hostel Warden. Where the Health Centre advises rest, the Health Centre shall issue a sick-diet authorisation and shall notify the Head of Department of the student concerned.

### HH-5.3 Fire Safety
Fire extinguishers are installed on every floor. Residents shall not obstruct corridors, staircases or fire exits. Fire drills are conducted once each semester and attendance at fire drills is compulsory for all residents.

### HH-5.4 Electrical Safety
Residents shall not tamper with electrical fittings or extend wiring. Faults shall be reported to the hostel maintenance desk.

---

## Section HH-6. Hostel Discipline

### HH-6.1 Disciplinary Authority
The Hostel Warden is the disciplinary authority in respect of hostel residents for matters arising within hostel premises. The Warden may issue a written warning, impose a fine, or recommend expulsion from the hostel to the Dean of Students.

### HH-6.2 Fines
Fines imposed under HH-6.1 shall not exceed five thousand rupees for a single instance and shall be recorded in the hostel disciplinary register.

### HH-6.3 Appeal
A resident may appeal a disciplinary decision of the Hostel Warden to the Dean of Students within seven working days of the decision. The decision of the Dean of Students on appeal is final in hostel matters.

### HH-6.4 Expulsion from the Hostel
A resident expelled from the hostel shall vacate the room within forty-eight hours of the order of expulsion. Expulsion from the hostel does not by itself affect the student's registration in the academic programme.

---

## Section HH-7. Facilities

### HH-7.1 Common Room
Each hostel block maintains a common room equipped with a television and indoor games. The common room remains open between 6:00 am and 10:30 pm.

### HH-7.2 Laundry
A laundry service operates on the campus. Collection and delivery are scheduled twice weekly for each block.

### HH-7.3 Internet
Wired and wireless internet access is provided in all hostel blocks. Residents shall use the network in accordance with the University's acceptable use policy. Bandwidth-intensive applications may be rate-limited during examination periods.

### HH-7.4 Reading Room
Each hostel maintains a reading room that remains open twenty-four hours during the end-semester examination period and between 6:00 am and 12:00 midnight at other times.

---

## Section HH-8. Security

### HH-8.1 Identity Card
Residents shall carry the University identity card at all times within hostel premises and shall produce it on demand to the Warden, security personnel or any authorised officer.

### HH-8.2 Personal Property
The University is not responsible for the loss of personal property from hostel rooms. Residents are advised to keep rooms locked when unoccupied and to deposit valuables in the hostel office safe.

### HH-8.3 Vehicles
Residents may keep bicycles in the designated bicycle stand. Motorised two-wheelers and four-wheelers require a parking permit issued by the security office on production of a valid driving licence and registration certificate.

---

## Section HH-9. Weekend and Vacation Procedures

### HH-9.1 Vacation Occupancy
Hostels remain open during the winter and summer vacations for students engaged in project work, internships or examination duties. A resident intending to remain in the hostel during a vacation shall register with the Hostel Warden not later than one week before the commencement of the vacation.

### HH-9.2 Vacation Mess
A limited mess service operates during vacations where the number of registered residents exceeds twenty. Where fewer than twenty residents register, the mess remains closed and residents make their own arrangements.

### HH-9.3 Weekend Leave
**A resident intending to travel home or elsewhere over a weekend shall record the intended absence in the hostel leave register not less than twenty-four (24) hours before departure**, together with contact details for the period of absence. The Hostel Warden shall countersign the entry before the resident departs.

### HH-9.4 Return from Vacation
Residents shall return to the hostel not later than the day preceding the commencement of classes as published in the academic calendar.

---

## Section HH-10. Withdrawal of Accommodation

### HH-10.1 Voluntary Withdrawal
A resident may withdraw from hostel accommodation by giving one month's written notice to the Hostel Warden. Hostel charges for the notice period remain payable.

### HH-10.2 Refund on Withdrawal
Refund of hostel charges on voluntary withdrawal is governed by the Fee Schedule.

### HH-10.3 Withdrawal for Non-Payment
Where hostel or mess charges remain unpaid for more than thirty days beyond the due date, the Hostel Warden may withdraw the allotment after giving the resident fifteen days' written notice to clear the dues.
