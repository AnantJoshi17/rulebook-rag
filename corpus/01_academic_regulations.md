# Academic Regulations — B.Tech Programme

**Document ID:** AR
**Version:** 2026.1
**Approved by:** Academic Council, 14 March 2026
**Applies to:** All students admitted to the four-year B.Tech programme from the 2023 batch onward.

---

## Section AR-1. Scope and Interpretation

### AR-1.1 Purpose
These regulations govern registration, attendance, internal assessment, end-semester examinations, promotion, grading and award of degree for the four-year Bachelor of Technology programme. They are to be read together with the Hostel Handbook (Document HH), the Merit Scholarship Policy (Document SP), the Fee Schedule (Document FS) and the Examination Committee Charter (Document EC).

### AR-1.2 Authority
Where any provision of these regulations is silent, the Dean of Academics shall refer the matter to the Academic Council. No officer of the University may create a new exemption, relaxation or penalty that is not written in these regulations or in a document formally approved by the Academic Council.

### AR-1.3 Definitions
For the purposes of these regulations:
- **"Semester"** means a teaching period of not fewer than fifteen working weeks, excluding the end-semester examination period.
- **"Registered course"** means a course for which the student has completed registration on the University portal before the registration deadline notified for that semester.
- **"Contact hour"** means one scheduled period of lecture, tutorial, laboratory or studio instruction of fifty minutes.
- **"Attendance percentage"** means the number of contact hours attended by a student in a registered course, expressed as a percentage of the total contact hours conducted in that course during the semester.
- **"Internal assessment"** means the aggregate of continuous evaluation components conducted during the semester, excluding the end-semester examination.
- **"End-semester examination"** means the written examination conducted at the close of the semester by the Controller of Examinations.
- **"Backlog"** means a registered course in which the student has been awarded grade F or grade I and which has not subsequently been cleared.

### AR-1.4 Academic Calendar
The academic year comprises two regular semesters, designated Odd and Even, and one optional Summer Term. The academic calendar for each year is published by the Dean of Academics not later than four weeks before the commencement of the Odd Semester. Dates published in the academic calendar are binding on all departments.

## Section AR-2. Registration

### AR-2.1 Registration Window
A student shall register for courses within the registration window notified in the academic calendar. The window shall remain open for not fewer than seven working days.

### AR-2.2 Late Registration
A student who fails to register within the notified window may register during the late registration period of five working days on payment of the late registration charge specified in the Fee Schedule. No registration shall be accepted after the close of the late registration period.

### AR-2.3 Credit Load
The normal credit load in a regular semester is twenty-two credits. A student may register for a minimum of eighteen credits and a maximum of twenty-six credits in a regular semester. Registration below eighteen credits requires the written approval of the Head of Department.

### AR-2.4 Course Withdrawal
A student may withdraw from a registered course up to the end of the sixth week of the semester. A withdrawn course is recorded on the transcript with grade W and is not counted in the computation of grade point average. Withdrawal is not permitted after the sixth week under any circumstances.

### AR-2.5 Repeat Registration
A student carrying a backlog shall register for that course in the next semester in which the course is offered. Repeat registration counts toward the credit load specified in AR-2.3.

## Section AR-3. Attendance

### AR-3.1 Obligation to Attend
Attendance at all scheduled contact hours is compulsory for every registered course. Faculty members shall record attendance for each contact hour and shall upload attendance to the University portal not later than the third working day following the class.

### AR-3.2 Minimum Attendance for Examination Eligibility
**A student must have attended not less than seventy-five per cent (75%) of the total contact hours conducted in a registered course in order to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination for that course.** This threshold is mandatory and applies to every registered course individually. A student who falls below seventy-five per cent attendance in a course shall be marked as detained in that course and shall be awarded grade I for that course.

### AR-3.3 Publication of Attendance
Cumulative attendance shall be published on the University portal at the close of the eighth week and again at the close of the thirteenth week of each semester. A student may raise an attendance correction request within five working days of publication. Requests raised after five working days shall not be entertained.

### AR-3.4 Attendance in Laboratory Courses
Laboratory contact hours are counted on the same basis as lecture contact hours. A student who misses a laboratory session may perform a make-up session with the written permission of the course instructor, and the make-up session shall be counted as attendance for the missed session.

### AR-3.5 Consequence of Detention
A student detained under AR-3.2 shall not be permitted to appear in the end-semester examination for that course, shall be awarded grade I, and shall be required to register for that course again in a subsequent semester. Internal assessment marks already earned in a course in which the student has been detained shall be carried forward to the repeat registration.

## Section AR-4. Internal Assessment

### AR-4.1 Components
Internal assessment in a theory course shall carry forty marks, distributed as two mid-semester tests of fifteen marks each and continuous evaluation of ten marks. Continuous evaluation may comprise quizzes, assignments, presentations or a term project at the discretion of the course instructor, and the scheme shall be announced in the first week of the semester.

### AR-4.2 Mid-Semester Tests
The two mid-semester tests shall be conducted in the seventh and thirteenth weeks of the semester respectively. Mid-semester test schedules are published by the Department.

### AR-4.3 Absence from a Mid-Semester Test
A student who is absent from a mid-semester test shall be awarded zero marks for that test, except where the absence is on account of documented illness. A student absent on account of illness shall submit a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner to the Head of Department within seven working days of the date of the test. On acceptance of the certificate, the Head of Department shall authorise a re-test, which shall be conducted before the close of the semester.

### AR-4.4 Disclosure of Internal Marks
Internal assessment marks shall be displayed to students not later than the fifteenth week of the semester. A student may seek a review of internal assessment marks by written application to the course instructor within three working days of display.

### AR-4.5 Minimum Internal Marks
There is no minimum internal assessment requirement for eligibility to appear in the end-semester examination. Eligibility is governed solely by attendance under Section AR-3 and by clearance of dues under the Fee Schedule.

---

## Section AR-5. End-Semester Examination

### AR-5.1 Conduct
The end-semester examination shall be conducted by the Controller of Examinations in accordance with the dates published in the academic calendar. The end-semester examination in a theory course carries sixty marks and is of three hours' duration.

### AR-5.2 Admit Card
No student shall be permitted to enter the examination hall without a valid admit card issued by the Controller of Examinations. Admit cards are issued only to students who satisfy the attendance requirement and who have no outstanding dues.

### AR-5.3 Absence from the End-Semester Examination
A student who is absent from an end-semester examination shall be awarded grade F in that course, except where the absence is covered by AR-5.4.

### AR-5.4 Medical Absence from the End-Semester Examination
Where a student is unable to appear in an end-semester examination on account of illness, hospitalisation or injury, and submits a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner to the Controller of Examinations within ten working days of the date of the examination, the student shall be permitted to appear in the make-up examination conducted under AR-5.5 and shall not be awarded grade F for that absence. This provision covers medical grounds only.

### AR-5.5 Make-Up Examination
A make-up examination shall be conducted not later than four weeks after the close of the regular end-semester examination period, for students whose absence has been approved under AR-5.4. Marks obtained in the make-up examination are treated as regular end-semester marks with no ceiling.

### AR-5.6 Unfair Means
A student found using unfair means in an examination shall be reported to the Examination Committee constituted under Document EC. Pending the decision of the Committee, the result of the student in that semester shall be withheld.

## Section AR-6. Grading and Promotion

### AR-6.1 Grade Scale
Grades are awarded on a ten-point scale: O (10), A+ (9), A (8), B+ (7), B (6), C (5), P (4), F (0), I (0), W (not counted).

### AR-6.2 Passing Standard
A student shall be deemed to have passed a course on obtaining grade P or higher, subject to obtaining not less than forty per cent of the marks in the end-semester examination component of that course.

### AR-6.3 Grade Point Average
The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and the Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) shall be computed as the credit-weighted average of grade points earned. Courses graded W are excluded from computation. Courses graded F or I are included with zero grade points until cleared.

### AR-6.4 Promotion to the Next Academic Year
A student shall be promoted from the first year to the second year on clearing not fewer than sixteen credits of the first-year curriculum. A student shall be promoted from the second year to the third year on clearing not fewer than forty-four credits, and from the third year to the fourth year on clearing not fewer than eighty credits.

### AR-6.5 Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within seven working days of publication of results, on payment of the re-evaluation charge specified in the Fee Schedule. Where re-evaluation results in a change of five marks or more, the revised marks shall stand and the re-evaluation charge shall be refunded.

### AR-6.6 Improvement Examination
A student who has passed a course with grade C or P may register once for an improvement examination in that course in a subsequent semester. The better of the two grades shall be retained on the transcript.

## Section AR-7. Exemptions and Relaxations

### AR-7.1 General Principle
Exemptions from the requirements of these regulations are granted only in the circumstances expressly set out in this Section. No exemption is granted by custom, precedent or oral assurance.

### AR-7.2 Representation of the University
A student who is absent from scheduled contact hours on account of participation in an inter-university sporting event, a national-level technical competition or a cultural event, in each case as the nominated representative of the University, shall have those contact hours treated as attended, provided the Dean of Students has approved the participation in writing before the event.

### AR-7.3 Duty Leave for Placement Activities
A student in the fourth year who is absent from scheduled contact hours on account of a campus placement process conducted by the Training and Placement Cell shall have those contact hours treated as attended, subject to a ceiling of ten contact hours per course per semester.

### AR-7.4 Medical Exemption from the Attendance Requirement
**Where a student has been unable to attend classes on account of illness, hospitalisation or injury, and submits a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner to the Head of Department, the student shall be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination provided the attendance in that course is not less than sixty-five per cent (65%).** The Head of Department shall verify the certificate and record the exemption on the University portal before the issue of admit cards.

### AR-7.5 Bereavement
A student who is absent on account of the death of a parent, sibling, grandparent or legal guardian shall have up to seven consecutive scheduled contact days treated as attended, on submission of documentary evidence to the Head of Department within fifteen working days of return.

### AR-7.6 Limits on Exemption
No combination of exemptions under AR-7.2, AR-7.3, AR-7.5 and AR-7.4 shall be applied so as to treat a student as having attended more than twenty per cent of the total contact hours conducted in a course.

## Section AR-8. Discipline in Academic Matters

### AR-8.1 Plagiarism
Submission of work that is not the student's own, without acknowledgement, constitutes plagiarism. A first instance of plagiarism in a course shall result in award of zero marks for the affected component. A second instance in the same programme shall be referred to the Examination Committee.

### AR-8.2 Proxy Attendance
A student who marks attendance on behalf of another student, and the student on whose behalf attendance is marked, shall each be awarded grade I in the course concerned and shall be reported to the Dean of Students.

### AR-8.3 Conduct in the Examination Hall
Possession of any electronic communication device in the examination hall, whether used or not, constitutes an examination offence and shall be reported under AR-5.6.

---

## Section AR-9. Records and Transcripts

### AR-9.1 Transcript
The transcript records all registered courses, grades awarded, credits earned, SGPA for each semester and cumulative CGPA. Courses graded W appear on the transcript with no grade points.

### AR-9.2 Provisional Certificate
A provisional certificate shall be issued within thirty working days of publication of the final semester result to a student who has cleared all registered courses and has no outstanding dues.

### AR-9.3 Correction of Records
An application for correction of a name, date of birth or programme record on University documents shall be made to the Registrar with supporting documentary evidence. Applications for correction received more than one year after issue of the document shall be considered by the Registrar on a case-by-case basis.

### AR-9.4 Retention of Answer Scripts
End-semester answer scripts shall be retained by the Controller of Examinations for a period of one year from the date of publication of results and shall be destroyed thereafter.

---

## Section AR-10. Degree Requirements

### AR-10.1 Credits
The B.Tech degree shall be awarded on the successful completion of one hundred and sixty credits of the prescribed curriculum, including all mandatory non-credit courses.

### AR-10.2 Maximum Duration
The maximum period permitted for completion of the B.Tech programme is seven years from the date of first registration. A student who does not complete the programme within seven years shall be removed from the rolls of the University.

### AR-10.3 Mandatory Internship
Every student shall complete an industrial internship of not fewer than six weeks between the sixth and seventh semesters and shall submit an internship report certified by the host organisation. The internship is a mandatory non-credit requirement for the award of the degree.

### AR-10.4 Class of Degree
A student obtaining a CGPA of 8.50 or above shall be awarded the degree with Distinction. A student obtaining a CGPA of 7.00 or above but below 8.50 shall be awarded the degree in First Class. A student obtaining a CGPA below 7.00 but not less than 5.50 shall be awarded the degree in Second Class.
